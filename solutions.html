Embedded Finance

Regulated finance inside your product

Embedded finance allows platforms to offer payments, wallets, lending, and payouts directly within their products. Talino enables enterprises to embed regulated financial capabilities without becoming a bank — and lets AI features inside your app settle real value under policy.
